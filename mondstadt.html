MONDSTADT

About the Nation of Freedom

Welcome to Mondstadt, the enchanting City of Freedom, where breathtaking landscapes, rich history, and refreshing breezes await! Located in the world of Teyvat in Genshin Impact, Mondstadt is known for its beautiful windmills, charming European-inspired architecture, lush green fields, and the peaceful waters of Cider Lake.
