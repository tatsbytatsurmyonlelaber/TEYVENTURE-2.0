QUICK ANSWERS

Is sending an inquiry the same as booking?

No. An inquiry only starts the conversation, and nothing is charged. When you are ready to book, open a destination page and use the booking button on the package you want.

What is the difference between a general inquiry and a custom itinerary?

Use a general inquiry for questions about packages, prices, bookings, or payments. Use a custom itinerary request when you want a trip planned around your own dates, group size, budget, and interests.

What should I include in a custom itinerary request?

The more we know, the better the plan: where you want to go, your dates (or a flexible window), who is travelling, a rough budget, and what you enjoy. Special needs such as dietary requirements or mobility go in the notes.

I saw a package I like. Can I ask about it?

Yes. Press the arrow button on any package card and this page opens with that package already attached to your inquiry. You can switch to a custom itinerary if you want to change it.
