FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

How much do Teyventure tour packages cost?

Our tour packages have different base prices depending on the destination and duration of the trip. Each package is designed to provide clear and convenient travel options for different types of travelers.

Do you accept group bookings?

Yes! We welcome group bookings for families, friends, and other organized groups. Group arrangements can be discussed with our team based on your preferred destination and itinerary.

How far in advance should I book a tour?

We recommend booking at least 2 months before your actual travel date to allow enough time for proper planning and arrangements. Bookings made only 1 week before the travel date are automatically non-refundable.

How can I inquire about a tour?

You can contact Teyventure through the inquiry details provided on our website. Tell us your preferred destination, travel dates, group size, and interests, and we'll help you explore your options.

What is your cancellation policy?

If you cancel 2 months before the actual travel date, 50% of the full payment is refundable. Cancellations made 1 month before the travel date are non-refundable, and bookings made only 1 week before the travel date are also automatically non-refundable.
